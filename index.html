About Mentor AI

Is this really Andrew Tate / Gary Vee / [insert name]? ▼
No — these are AI-powered characters trained on each mentor's public content, speech patterns, and philosophy. They're designed to sound authentic and give advice in that mentor's style, but they are not the actual people.
Do I need an account to use it? ▼
No account needed. Your conversations and settings are saved in your browser's localStorage. Everything is local to your device — we don't store anything on a server.
Is Mentor AI free? ▼
Yes. The app is free to use with a Groq API key (they offer 14,400 requests/day on their free tier). You can also use OpenRouter, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or any custom API endpoint.
Can I add my own mentors? ▼
Yes. Mentors are defined in a simple JSON file. You can edit `characters.json` to add, remove, or modify any mentor's name, tag, greeting, personality prompt, and avatar. No code changes needed.
Which AI providers are supported? ▼
Groq (default), OpenRouter, Google Gemini, OpenAI, and any custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. You can switch providers in the settings panel — your API key stays in your browser.
Does each mentor remember our conversations? ▼
Yes. Each mentor keeps its own conversation history stored locally. When you switch between mentors, their context is preserved. You can also clear individual conversations anytime.
